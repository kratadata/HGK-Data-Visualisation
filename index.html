    Individual Work Day

    Prepare

    Collect 20 to 30 images that you would use a training data to train LoRa model.

      Make sure that the images:
    • Have a cohesive visual style e.x. similar colors, composition, mood, or aesthetic
    • Are in high enough quality to analyze visual features
    • Use `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, or `.png` format
    • Are resized to max. 2048 px (either in width or in height)
      Good examples:
    • 30 illustration with similar shading, linework and style e.x. doodles
    • 30 architectural photos of e.g., the same building, the same architect
    • 30 images of the clothes from the same fashion brand
